The **Meghalaya Tourism Homestay Scheme**, officially scaled and notified as the **Chief Minister's Meghalaya Homestay Mission (CMMHM)**, is a flagship capital-subsidy and entrepreneurship initiative of the **Tourism Department, Government of Meghalaya**, implemented in active institutional partnership with the **Commerce and Industries Department (through the District Commerce and Industry Centres – DCICs)**, the **Meghalaya Investment Promotion Authority (MIPA)**, and participating commercial/regional rural banks (notably **Meghalaya Rural Bank**).

The scheme was designed to address high tourist accommodation deficits across key eco-tourism circuits in the state without building large ecological footprints or commercial concrete mega-structures. By offering a capital and credit-linked subsidy of **up to 70%** (and up to **80% for Persons with Disabilities**), the Government of Meghalaya empowers rural families, youth, and local micro-entrepreneurs to build, expand, and operate standardized, sustainable homestays and rural experiential tourism facilities.

The Mission targets the commissioning of **3,000 homestay units** and the generation of over **15,000 direct and indirect sustainable livelihoods across Meghalaya by 2028**.

- **Initial Formulation (Meghalaya Tourism Homestay Scheme / MTDIPS convergence):** Introduced as a state initiative leveraging convergence with the Government of India’s **Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)**. It introduced the dual 35% + 35% credit-linked subsidy formula (35% under PMEGP + 35% top-up from the Meghalaya Tourism Department = 70% total capital subsidy).
- **Scale-Up into the Chief Minister’s Meghalaya Homestay Mission:** Expanded on World Tourism Day (September 2025) under Chief Minister **Conrad K. Sangma**, adding dedicated financial envelopes for:
  - **Component 1:** Development of New Homestays (standard units up to ₹10 lakh).
  - **Component 2:** Upgradation of Existing Operational Homestays (grants up to ₹2 lakh).
  - **Component 3:** Development of Boutique Homestays (minimum 10-room facilities with project costs starting at ₹80 lakh, with special incentives for the National Games).
- **Key Implementing Agencies:**
  - **Directorate of Tourism, Government of Meghalaya:** State nodal agency responsible for policy, DPR evaluation, design approval, site inspection, and release of state capital subsidies/EMI assistance.
  - **District Commerce and Industry Centres (DCIC):** District-level partner agencies facilitating enterprise registration, PMEGP credit-linkage vetting, and industrial subsidy facilitation.
  - **Partner Financial Institutions:** Meghalaya Rural Bank (MRB), State Bank of India (SBI), and designated commercial lenders providing credit facilities.
  - **Capacity Building Partner:** Institute of Hotel Management (IHM), Shillong, providing mandatory training in housekeeping, hygiene, front-office hospitality, and local ethnic culinary skills.
  - **Digital Partner:** Meghalaya Tourism Information & Management Engine (**M-TIME** Portal / Meghalaya Tourism App).

- **Standard Project Cost Ceiling:** Admissible benchmark cost up to **₹10,00,000 (Rupees Ten Lakhs)**.
- **Subsidy Rate:**
  - **General / Special Category (ST/SC/Women/Youth):** **Up to 70%** of project cost, capped at **₹7,00,000**.
  - **Persons with Disabilities (PwD) Category:** Enhanced subsidy of **up to 80%**, subject to a maximum cap of **₹8,00,000**.
- **Credit-Linked Debt-Equity Structure:**
  - **Beneficiary Promoter Contribution:** Minimum **5%** of the project cost for Special Categories (ST/SC/Women), or **10%** for general non-priority applicants.
  - **Bank Loan:** Up to **90%–95%** of the project cost sanctioned as a commercial term loan.
- **Repayment Terms:**
  - **Moratorium Period:** **12 months (1 year)** from the date of initial loan disbursement during which no principal repayment is required.
  - **Repayment Tenure:** Total loan tenure of up to **7 years (84 months)**.
  - **EMI Subsidy Disbursement Mechanism:** The Tourism Department deposits its 35% subsidy portion directly with the partner financing bank as **EMI support during the first 24 to 36 months** (reducing the cash burden on the entrepreneur to an average effective monthly out-of-pocket EMI of only ~₹6,000/month). PMEGP margin money is adjusted according to central scheme lock-in guidelines.
- **Upper-Cap Expansion Option (Loans up to ₹20 Lakhs):**
  - While PMEGP permits service sector tourism loans up to ₹20 Lakhs, the Meghalaya Tourism Department’s capital subsidy under Component 1 remains capped at **35% of ₹10 Lakhs (₹3,50,000)**. Any loan component above ₹10 Lakhs is serviced solely by the borrower under standard bank terms.

- **Target Beneficiaries:** Existing, actively operating homestays in Meghalaya needing quality enhancement, linen upgrades, solar heating, sanitary improvements, or interior refits.
- **Grant Outlay:** **₹50,000 per room**, subject to a maximum ceiling of **₹2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs)** per homestay unit.
- **Special Green/Renewable Energy Provision:** Up to ₹1,00,000 allocation within the scheme ecosystem for solar lighting/water heating and eco-friendly infrastructure integration.

- **Target Facility:** Homestay accommodation facilities with **at least 10 guest rooms** (new construction or major brownfield expansion).
- **Minimum Project Cost:** **₹80,00,000 (Rupees Eighty Lakhs)**.
- **Financing Ratio:** Structured on a **90:10 Debt-Equity Model** (borrower contributes 10% equity / minimum ₹8 Lakhs; balance financed by bank term loan).
- **Incentive Ceiling:** Maximum state incentive capped at **₹15,00,000**, structured as:
  - **₹10 Lakhs:** Standard Government EMI support for the first 12 months.
  - **₹5 Lakhs:** *National Games Accommodation Bonus* for units completed on or before prescribed milestones in host clusters (Shillong, Tura, Jowai).
- **Loan Tenure:** 1-year moratorium followed by a **9-year repayment period**. Additional state benefits include **State GST (SGST) reimbursement** for new units and a **2% annual interest subvention for 7 years** in East Khasi Hills, West Garo Hills, and West Jaintia Hills.

1. **Domicile:** Must be an Indian citizen and a **permanent resident/domicile of Meghalaya**.
2. **Age & Literacy:** Must be at least **18 years of age** and functionally literate.
3. **One Unit Per Family:** Only **one individual per family/household** is eligible to receive financial assistance under the scheme.
4. **Credit Record:** Must not be a defaulter with any commercial bank, regional rural bank, cooperative credit institution, or state financial agency.
5. **Mandatory Capacity Building:** The owner/beneficiary must undergo mandatory hospitality, sanitation, and culinary training conducted by the Department of Tourism or partner institutions like IHM Shillong.

- **Ownership / Title:** The applicant must either:
  - Be the rightful legal owner of the proposed land/property, **OR**
  - Hold a **registered lease deed** for the property with an unexpired validity of at least **7 years** (for standard homestays) or **15 years** (for Boutique units).
- **Local NOC:** Must possess a valid **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** from the local traditional authority (Rangbah Shnong / Dorbar Shnong in Khasi/Jaintia Hills, or Nokma in Garo Hills) or the competent municipal/urban board.
- **Road Accessibility:** Property must be accessible by road or well-maintained pathways connected to recognized tourism circuits.

- **Active Operational History:** The homestay must have been operational and welcoming guests for **at least 6 months** prior to application.
- **Guest Footfall Proof:** The applicant’s physical or electronic guest register must reflect a minimum of **50 recorded bed-nights**.
- **Mandatory Portal Listing:** The homestay must be officially registered and actively listed on the **Meghalaya M-TIME Portal**.

To preserve indigenous vernacular architecture and avoid unsightly haphazard construction, the Department of Tourism, Government of Meghalaya, mandated that:
- Every applicant developing a new homestay under Component 1 **must adopt one of the 33 approved standard homestay designs** prepared by the Tourism Department.
- These pre-engineered architectural layouts incorporate local materials (bamboo, treated pinewood, stone masonry, pitched roof designs suitable for heavy monsoon rainfall) while guaranteeing standardized guest room dimensions, en-suite western sanitary plumbing, adequate ventilation, and mandatory tourist safety norms.
- Adopting a pre-approved design eliminates the requirement for individual private architect fees, expedites structural vetting by the PWD/Tourism engineering wing, and guarantees smooth bank loan underwriting.

1. **Online Submission:** Applicant registers on the Meghalaya Tourism / Investment Promotion portal (`https://invest.meghalaya.gov.in` or via `https://meghalayaone.gov.in`) using their Mobile Number / EPIC ID.
2. **Application Track Selection:** The applicant selects *Chief Minister’s Meghalaya Homestay Mission*, picks their component (New Build / Upgradation / Boutique), fills the application, uploads land and KYC documents, and chooses their preferred standard architectural model.
3. **Joint Scrutiny (DCIC & Tourism Officer):** The application is forwarded to the local District Commerce & Industry Centre (DCIC) and the District Tourism Officer (DTO).
4. **Physical Site Inspection:** A joint physical inspection verifies road access, land authenticity, scenic suitability, and local Dorbar NOC validity.
5. **Task Force Recommendation & Bank Credit Sanction:** The District Task Force Committee recommends the qualified application to the selected partner bank (e.g., Meghalaya Rural Bank). The bank conducts internal credit appraisal and issues a formal **Sanction Letter**.
6. **Loan Disbursement & Construction:** The loan is disbursed in phased, construction-linked installments following physical milestone verification by the Tourism Department’s engineering wing.
7. **Subsidy Release & EMI Offsetting:** The Tourism Department deposits its capital subsidy share with the financing bank branch to offset the entrepreneur’s monthly EMI during the moratorium and initial operational years.
8. **M-TIME Onboarding & Launch:** Upon completion, the homestay receives its official Tourist Registration Certificate, is onboarded onto the Meghalaya Tourism booking ecosystem, and its operators complete the mandatory IHM training module.

The Directorate of Tourism reserves the unconditional authority to recall subsidies, cancel registrations, or trigger loan recovery proceedings under the following circumstances:
- **Misrepresentation or Fraud:** Submitting forged land titles, fake Dorbar NOCs, or falsified guest registers.
- **Unauthorized Design Deviation:** Significantly deviating from the approved 33 standard architectural layouts without prior written approval from the Tourism Department.
- **Commercial Divergence:** Utilizing the subsidized facility for private long-term rental housing, retail godowns, or purposes other than tourist accommodation.
- **Sub-Leasing / Transfer:** Selling, leasing, or transferring ownership of the subsidized homestay to a third party prior to the completion of the 7-year loan tenure.
- **Willful Default:** Defaulting on promoter-share EMI obligations, in which case the bank and the Government of Meghalaya initiate recovery under the Meghalaya Public Demands Recovery Act.

- **PMEGP (Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme):** Forms the foundational central credit-linked subsidy base (35% margin money support) under KVIC/DIC guidelines.
- **UNNATI (Uttar Poorva Transformative Industrialization Scheme):** Industrial development subventions convergeable for larger Boutique Homestay units.
- **PM-Surya Ghar / State Green Energy Incentives:** Complementary rooftop solar integration for rural off-grid homestays.
