The **Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)** is a flagship state-funded educational welfare programme under the **Education Department, Government of Meghalaya**. It provides a fixed annual financial grant of **₹6,000/- (Rupees six thousand only) per annum** to every eligible student who is a domicile of Meghalaya pursuing full-time, regular post-matriculation studies—ranging from **Class XI up to the Ph.D. level**—in recognised educational institutions located either within or outside Meghalaya.

Unlike means-tested or quota-restricted welfare schemes, CMSS operates as a universal post-matric educational support grant for Meghalaya's youth:
- It has **no family income ceiling** (Section 5 of the scheme guidelines).
- It has **no caste, tribe, or community quota** and **no merit-list cap** on the number of beneficiaries.
- It can be availed **in addition to** other state or central scholarships applied for through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or similar portals (Section 4(e) of the scheme guidelines).

At the state level, the **Education Department, Government of Meghalaya** is the notifying policy department, while the **Directorate of Higher and Technical Education (DHTE), Meghalaya, Shillong** is the nodal implementing authority (referred to as "the Directorate" in the scheme guidelines). All applications (Fresh and Renewal) are processed exclusively online through the **Meghalaya One Portal** (`https://meghalayaone.gov.in`), and scholarship amounts are disbursed directly into students' bank accounts via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**.

- **Official Scheme Name:** *Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)* (notified initially as the *Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme, 2025*).
- **Commonly Used Names / Abbreviations:** CMSS, CMSS Meghalaya, Meghalaya CM Scholarship Scheme, Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme Meghalaya.
- **Distinction from the Umbrella Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students (NSP):**
  Because both schemes support post-matric students in Meghalaya, applicants frequently confuse them. They are two completely separate programmes run on different portals:

| Parameter | Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS) | Umbrella Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Funding & Authority** | 100% State-funded by the **Government of Meghalaya** (Education Department / DHTE) | Centrally Sponsored Scheme by the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Govt. of India** (implemented via State) |
| **Scholarship Amount** | **₹6,000 per annum (Fixed flat rate)** for all levels from Class XI to Ph.D. | **Variable by course group** (covers compulsory non-refundable tuition/academic fees plus maintenance allowance) |
| **Family Income Limit** | **No income ceiling** (open to all income brackets) | Parents'/guardians' annual income must **not exceed ₹2.50 lakh per annum** |
| **Eligible Community** | **All domiciles (permanent residents)** of Meghalaya | **Scheduled Tribe (ST)** students only |
| **Application Portal** | **Meghalaya One Portal** (`https://meghalayaone.gov.in`) | **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** (`https://scholarships.gov.in`) |
| **Mutual Exclusivity** | **Stackable:** Can be availed *in addition to* the NSP Umbrella Scholarship | Eligible ST students can apply for and receive both on their respective portals |

- **Distinction from Pre-Matric and Central PMSS:** CMSS does not cover Classes I to X (which fall under Pre-Matric scholarships) and is distinct from the central *Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)* for wards of Ex-Servicemen and CAPF personnel.

As verbatim stated in the official scheme guidelines (Section 3), the objectives of the Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme are:

1. **(a) Educational Empowerment:** *"To empower eligible students of Meghalaya to pursue quality education from post-matriculation to doctorate level by providing financial assistance."*
2. **(b) Transparent Direct Delivery:** *"To ensure timely, direct, and error-free delivery of scholarships to the targeted beneficiaries."*

- **No Income Ceiling (Section 5):** There is no family income limit for applicants under CMSS. No income certificate is asked for or required during application.
- **Uniform Fixed Rate with Future Differential Revision Provision (Section 6):** Each eligible student is awarded a fixed scholarship of **₹6,000/- per annum**. The guidelines provide that the Government of Meghalaya may revise this amount from time to time by notification, and such a revision may either maintain a uniform rate across all courses or prescribe differential rates based on the nature and level of the course pursued. Currently, the uniform ₹6,000/year rate applies across all levels.
- **Permitted alongside Other Scholarships (Section 4(e)):** Students can avail of CMSS in addition to other scholarships applied for through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or similar portals.
- **No Cap on Beneficiary Count or Renewal Years:** The guidelines impose no quota limit on the number of recipients per institution/course and place no cap on the number of academic years a continuing student may renew the scholarship, provided they remain enrolled in a recognised full-time post-matric course and meet the academic and conduct criteria.
- **Not a Legal Right / Subject to Fund Availability (Section 10 & Section 12):**
  - Disbursement into the beneficiary's bank account is expressly **subject to the availability of funds**.
  - Section 12 specifies that *"the grant of financial assistance under this Scheme shall not be construed as a matter of right."* It remains subject to fulfilment of eligibility criteria, verification of credentials, availability of funds, and any other conditions specified by the Directorate.
- **Power to Relax Guidelines (Section 11):** Any relaxation or deviation from the published guidelines in exceptional circumstances is reserved strictly to the State Government with the **prior approval of the Chief Minister**. Individual institutions or district offices cannot waive eligibility rules locally.

To be eligible for CMSS, a student must meet all of the following requirements:
1. **Domicile:** The student must be a **domicile (permanent resident) of Meghalaya**.
2. **Course Level:** The student must be enrolled in a post-matriculation programme: **Class XI, Class XII, Diploma courses, Undergraduate (Graduation), Postgraduate (Post-Graduation), M.Phil., or Ph.D. level**.
3. **Course Mode:** The student must be pursuing a **full-time, regular course**.
4. **Institution Location & Recognition:** The student must be enrolled in a **recognised institution located either within Meghalaya or outside the State** (anywhere in India), falling under one of the nine approved institutional categories (see Section 7).
5. **Minimum Academic Marks Requirement:** The student must have secured **at least 35% marks in the preceding academic year**.
6. **Personal Banking & Contact Details:** The student must possess a **valid bank account** (in the student's own name), an active **mobile number**, and a valid **email ID**.

- **Non-Domiciles:** Students who are not permanent residents/domiciles of Meghalaya.
- **Part-Time, Distance, or Correspondence Students:** Under Section 4(d), students pursuing part-time courses, distance learning, or correspondence courses are strictly ineligible, regardless of the university or board.
- **Unrecognised Institutions:** Students studying at unrecognised private institutes, non-affiliated colleges, or private tutorial/coaching centres.
- **Pre-Matric Students:** Students studying in Class X or below.
- **Below 35% Marks:** Students who failed to secure at least 35% marks in their preceding academic year's examination.

Under Section 4(c) of the official guidelines, the scholarship is available **only** to students pursuing full-time regular courses in the following nine statutory categories of recognised institutions (within or outside Meghalaya):

- **i) Government Institutions:** Government institutes, colleges, and universities (Central or State Government).
- **ii) Institutions of National Importance:** IITs, NITs, IIMs, AIIMS, IIITs, NIDs, and other statutory Institutions of National Importance.
- **iii) UGC-Recognised Universities & Colleges:** Central Universities, State Universities, autonomous colleges recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), and institutions recognised under **Section 2(f) and/or Section 12(B)** of the UGC Act.
- **iv) Deemed Universities:** Institutions declared as Deemed-to-be Universities.
- **v) Affiliated Private Universities & Institutions:** Private universities and private institutions duly affiliated with recognised Central or State Universities.
- **vi) Recognised Schools for Higher Secondary:** Government, Government-aided, and recognised Private schools (**specifically for Classes XI and XII**).
- **vii) Diploma-Granting Institutions:** State Government or Union Territory (UT)-recognised diploma-granting polytechnics and institutions.
- **viii) Vocational Training Institutes:** Vocational training institutes (such as ITIs) affiliated with the **National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT)**.
- **ix) Regulatory-Body Recognised Professional Institutions:** Institutions recognised by statutory regulatory bodies such as the Medical Council of India / National Medical Commission (**MCI/NMC**), All India Council for Technical Education (**AICTE**), Indian Nursing Council (INC), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), Bar Council of India (BCI), Council of Architecture (CoA), etc.

Instead of requiring applicants to obtain a separate paper Domicile Certificate from district authorities, CMSS establishes Meghalaya domicile credentials through four accepted household/citizen identity documents (Section 8).

Applicants are required to upload the following documents at the time of online application:
1. **(a) Identity / Domicile Credential Proof — Any ONE of the following four documents:**
   - **MHIS Card** (Meghalaya Health Insurance Scheme Card) of the parents/guardian.
   - **MGNREGS Job Card** of the parents/guardian.
   - **EPIC (Election Photo ID Card / Voter ID)** of the applicant or parents/guardian.
   - **PDS (Ration) Card** of the household.
   *(Official Guideline Note: Submission of more than one of the above four documents is explicitly encouraged, as it facilitates faster verification of credentials.)*
2. **(b) Mark Sheet of the previous academic year** (confirming at least 35% marks).
3. **(c) Recent passport-size photograph** of the applicant.
4. **(d) Bonafide Certificate** issued, signed, and sealed by the **Head of the Institution**.
5. **Bank Passbook (first page) or Cancelled Cheque** (required while entering bank account details—Account Number, Bank Name, and IFSC code—to verify that the account is in the applicant's name).

Applications are accepted **online only** via the official **Meghalaya One Portal** (`https://meghalayaone.gov.in`). There is no offline/paper form, and applications cannot be submitted on NSP.

1. **Step 1:** Visit the official Meghalaya One website (`https://meghalayaone.gov.in`) and click on the **“Citizen”** option.
2. **Step 2:** Click on **“Registration for New User”** to begin creating your citizen account.
3. **Step 3:** Select your **Authentication ID** from the available options on the portal, such as **PDS (Ration Card)** or **Election Photo ID Card (EPIC)**.
4. **Step 4:** Enter your card number, select your name from the displayed household/electoral list, and click **“Next”** to continue.
5. **Step 5:** Your EPIC ID details will appear on the screen. If the displayed details are correct, click **“Accept.”** If the details are incorrect or rejected, manually enter your EPIC number and proceed.
6. **Step 6:** Click **“Validate”** to verify the entered EPIC number and complete OTP verification on your mobile number/email, then click **“Next”** to move to the application form.
7. **Step 7:** Fill in all required details in the CMSS application form, upload the necessary documents, review all information, and click **“Submit.”** Save the generated **Unique Application Number** for future status tracking.

1. **Step 1:** Visit `https://meghalayaone.gov.in`, click **“Sign In”**, and choose your preferred sign-in method from **Mobile Number, PDS, EPIC, or Member ID**.
2. **Step 2:** Enter your User ID and Password (or OTP) and click **“Login”** to access your citizen dashboard.
3. **Step 3:** Click on **“Apply for Services/Scheme”** (or go to the **“Services”** section) and select **Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)**.
4. **Step 4:** Choose your application track: **“Fresh”** (if applying for the first time) or **“Renewal”** (if you received CMSS previously—always use the same Meghalaya One account used for your Fresh application).
5. **Step 5:** Fill in personal, academic, and bank account details, upload clear PDF/image scans of the required documents, verify every entry, and click **“Submit.”**

- Once submitted, applications undergo verification by institutional and state-level verifiers under the Directorate of Higher and Technical Education (DHTE).
- If an application has a missing seal, unreadable scan, or data mismatch, it is flagged as **“Defective”** on the portal rather than rejected outright.
- The student must log back into the **same Meghalaya One account** used to apply, view the defect notice on the dashboard, upload the corrected document/information, and re-submit **before the Defect Compliance Deadline** (e.g., **4 December 2026** for the 2026–27 cycle).

1. **Blurry or Incomplete Uploads:** Uploading unreadable marksheets, cropped certificates, or illegible identity cards.
2. **Bank Account Name Mismatch or Invalid Details:** Providing a parent's/relative's bank account instead of an account in the student's own name, or entering an incorrect IFSC code / dormant account.
3. **Incomplete Bonafide Certificate:** Uploading a Bonafide Certificate that lacks the official seal/stamp or signature of the Head of the Institution, or uploading last year's Bonafide Certificate for a Renewal application.
4. **Selecting the Wrong Application Track:** Filing a "Fresh" application when the student is actually a "Renewal" beneficiary (or vice versa), or creating a duplicate Meghalaya One account instead of renewing through the original account.
5. **Last-Minute Submission Errors:** Rushing form entry just before the closing deadline and entering mismatched marks percentages or roll numbers.

Under Section 9 of the official guidelines, the **Directorate of Higher and Technical Education (DHTE)** reserves the right to cancel a scholarship under the following three statutory conditions:

- **(a) Misconduct or Disciplinary Action:** If the student is involved in misconduct or disciplinary proceedings, as reported by the concerned educational institution.
- **(b) Academic Requirement Floor (< 35% Marks):** If the student fails to secure at least **35% marks** in the preceding academic year (in the notified guidelines, the 35% academic floor operates under the Cancellation/Continuation rules alongside institutional conduct).
- **(c) Ineligibility Detected at Any Stage & Refund Clause:** If the student is found to be ineligible at any stage, either during verification or thereafter (post-disbursement). **In such cases, the student shall be liable to refund the entire scholarship amount received.**
