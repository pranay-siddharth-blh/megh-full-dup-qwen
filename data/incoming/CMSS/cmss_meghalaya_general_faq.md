1. What is the Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)?
The Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS), under the **Education Department, Government of Meghalaya**, provides annual financial support of **₹6,000** to students domiciled in Meghalaya from **Class XI up to the Ph.D. level** to help them pursue full-time, regular post-matriculation education in recognised institutions within or outside the state.

2. Which department and directorate implement CMSS in Meghalaya?
The scheme is notified by the **Education Department, Government of Meghalaya**, and is implemented by the **Directorate of Higher and Technical Education (DHTE), Meghalaya, Shillong**.

3. When was CMSS announced and formally notified?
CMSS was announced by Chief Minister **Conrad K. Sangma** on **5 March 2025** in the Meghalaya State Budget for 2025–26 (projected to cover ~80,000 youth with an annual outlay of ~₹48 crore). It was formally notified by the Education Department (under Commissioner & Secretary Vijay Kumar Mantri) on **25 August 2025**, with the first application cycle opening on **1 September 2025**.

4. What are the official objectives of the scheme?
According to the scheme guidelines, CMSS has two core objectives:
- **(a)** To empower eligible students of Meghalaya to pursue quality education from post-matriculation to doctorate level by providing financial assistance.
- **(b)** To ensure timely, direct, and error-free delivery of scholarships to the targeted beneficiaries.

5. How much scholarship amount is awarded under CMSS?
Each eligible student receives a fixed scholarship of **₹6,000/- per annum (Rupees six thousand only per year)**. There is no difference by class or course—a Class XI student and a Ph.D. scholar receive the same ₹6,000 annual grant. The amount may be revised from time to time by the Government of Meghalaya (either maintaining a uniform rate or prescribing course-wise differential rates by notification).

6. Who is eligible to apply for the scholarship?
The scholarship is available to students who:
- Are **domiciles (permanent residents) of Meghalaya**.
- Are enrolled in **full-time, regular courses** from **Class XI to Ph.D. level** (including Class XI, Class XII, Diploma, Graduation, Post-Graduation, M.Phil., and Ph.D.).
- Are studying in a **recognised institution** located either within Meghalaya or outside the state.
- Have secured **at least 35% marks** in the preceding academic year.
- Possess a **valid bank account** (in their own name), an active **mobile number**, and a valid **email ID**.

7. Is there an income limit (family income ceiling) to apply for CMSS?
**No.** Under Section 5 of the scheme guidelines, there is **no income ceiling** for applicants under this scheme. Students from all family income levels are eligible, and no income certificate is required.

8. Is CMSS restricted only to Scheduled Tribe (ST) students?
**No.** CMSS is open to **all permanent-resident (domicile) students of Meghalaya** regardless of tribe, caste, or community.

9. Can students receive CMSS along with other scholarships (such as NSP scholarships)?
**Yes.** Students can avail of CMSS **in addition to** other scholarships applied for through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or similar portals.

10. How is CMSS different from the Umbrella Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students?
They are two distinct schemes on different portals:
- **CMSS:** Funded 100% by the **Meghalaya State Government**, pays a flat **₹6,000/year**, has **no income limit**, covers **all Meghalaya domiciles** (Class XI–Ph.D.), and is applied for on the **Meghalaya One Portal** (`meghalayaone.gov.in`).
- **Umbrella Post-Matric ST Scholarship:** Funded by the **Central Government (Ministry of Tribal Affairs)** + State, pays **variable course-wise fees and maintenance allowances**, has a **₹2.50 lakh/year family income limit**, covers **only ST students**, and is applied for on the **National Scholarship Portal (NSP)** (`scholarships.gov.in`).
Eligible ST students can apply for and receive **both** scholarships on their respective portals.

11. Are part-time, distance learning, or correspondence students eligible?
**No.** Students pursuing part-time or correspondence (distance education) courses are strictly ineligible for this scholarship.

12. What is the minimum academic requirement to apply or renew?
Students must have secured **at least 35% marks in the preceding academic year** to be eligible and to continue receiving (renewing) the scholarship.

13. Can students studying outside Meghalaya apply?
**Yes.** Students who are domiciles of Meghalaya and enrolled in full-time regular courses at recognised institutions **outside the state** (anywhere in India) are fully eligible.

14. Can students from private schools, private colleges, or deemed universities apply?
**Yes**, provided the institution falls under the scheme's nine recognised categories:
1. Government institutes, colleges, and universities.
2. Institutions of National Importance (IITs, NITs, IIMs, AIIMS, etc.).
3. Central/State Universities, UGC-recognised autonomous colleges, and institutions recognised under **Section 2(f) and/or 12(B)** of the UGC Act.
4. Deemed Universities.
5. Private universities/institutions affiliated with recognised Central or State Universities.
6. Government, Government-aided, and recognised Private schools (**specifically for Classes XI and XII**).
7. State/UT-recognised diploma-granting institutions (polytechnics).
8. **NCVT-affiliated** vocational training institutes (ITIs).
9. Institutions recognised by statutory regulatory bodies such as **MCI/NMC, AICTE, INC, PCI, BCI**, etc.

15. Where and how do I apply for CMSS? Is there an offline form?
Applications are accepted **online only** through the official **Meghalaya One Portal** (`https://meghalayaone.gov.in`). There is no offline or paper form, and you must **not** apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) for this scheme.

16. How does a first-time user register on the Meghalaya One Portal?
1. Visit `https://meghalayaone.gov.in` and click on **“Citizen”**.
2. Click **“Registration for New User”**.
3. Select your **Authentication ID** from the options (such as **PDS Ration Card** or **EPIC Voter ID**).
4. Enter the card number, select your name from the displayed list, and click **“Next”**.
5. Accept or manually validate your EPIC details, verify with an OTP, and complete registration.
6. Log in using your **Mobile Number, PDS, EPIC, or Member ID**, go to **“Apply for Services/Scheme”**, select **Chief Minister's Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)**, choose **Fresh**, fill out the form, upload documents, and click **“Submit”**.

17. What documents are required for a Fresh Application?
First-time applicants must upload clear scans of:
1. **Identity / Domicile Credential Proof** — Any **one** of the following (submitting more than one is encouraged to speed up verification):
   - **MHIS Card** of the parents/guardian
   - **MGNREGS Job Card** of the parents/guardian
   - **EPIC (Voter ID)** of the applicant or parents/guardian
   - **PDS (Ration) Card**
2. **Mark sheet** of the previous academic year ($\ge 35\%$ marks).
3. **Bonafide Certificate** issued by the Head of the Institution (with official seal and signature).
4. **Recent passport-size photograph**.
5. **Bank passbook (first page) or cancelled cheque** showing the student's own bank account number and IFSC code.

18. Do I need a separate paper Domicile Certificate to apply?
**No.** The scheme guidelines do not ask for a separate Domicile Certificate. Instead, uploading any one (or more) of the four household/identity documents—Parents' MHIS Card, Parents' MGNREGS Job Card, Applicant/Parents' EPIC (Voter ID), or PDS Ration Card—establishes your Meghalaya credentials.

19. How do I renew my CMSS scholarship, and what documents are needed for Renewal?
Renewal is not automatic; continuing students must log into the **same Meghalaya One account** used for their Fresh application and select the **Renewal** track during the annual application window.
- **If continuing in the same institution:** Upload (1) Latest previous year's marksheet ($\ge 35\%$), (2) Fresh Bonafide Certificate from your current institution, (3) Recent passport-size photograph, and (4) Bank passbook/cancelled cheque **only if your bank details have changed**.
- **If you changed your institution (e.g., moved from Class XII to College, or UG to PG):** Upload (1) Latest previous year's marksheet ($\ge 35\%$), (2) Fresh Bonafide Certificate issued by the **new institution**, (3) Recent passport-size photograph, and (4) Bank passbook/cancelled cheque **only if your bank details have changed**.

20. How is the scholarship disbursed, and when is it paid?
The ₹6,000 scholarship is disbursed directly into the student's bank account via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)**. Neither the DHTE advertisement nor the scheme guidelines fix a specific calendar date for payment; disbursement occurs after state-level verification is completed and is expressly **subject to the availability of budget funds**.

22. What does a "Defect Notice" mean, and how do I fix it?
If state-level verifiers spot an issue with your application (such as a blurry marksheet scan, a Bonafide Certificate missing the principal's seal, or a bank name mismatch), your application is flagged as **Defective**. You must log into the **same Meghalaya One account** you used to apply, open the flagged application, upload the corrected document(s), and re-submit before the defect compliance deadline (**4 December 2026** for the 2026–27 session).

23. Under what circumstances can the Directorate cancel a scholarship or demand a refund?
Under Section 9 of the guidelines, the Directorate of Higher and Technical Education (DHTE) reserves the right to cancel the scholarship if:
1. **(a) Misconduct:** The student is involved in misconduct or disciplinary proceedings, as reported by the concerned institution.
2. **(b) Low Marks:** The student fails to secure at least **35% marks** in the preceding academic year.
3. **(c) Ineligibility & Refund Liability:** The student is found to be ineligible at any stage, either during verification or thereafter. **In such cases, the student is legally liable to refund the entire scholarship amount received.**

24. Is the CMSS scholarship a legal entitlement (matter of right)?
**No.** Section 12 of the guidelines states that the grant of financial assistance under CMSS **shall not be construed as a matter of right**. It is subject to fulfilling eligibility conditions, verification, availability of state funds, and conditions specified by DHTE. Furthermore, any relaxation of the rules in exceptional cases requires the prior approval of the Chief Minister.
