REPRODUCED. It is worse than v1 said, and two parts of the v1 fix would have broken pinned tests.
The issue_resolved_scope_missing returns "no problem" for each of these:
  • WEST GARO HILLS against ='SOUTH WEST GARO HILLS', ILIKE '%WEST%', or a literal that appears only in the SELECT list;
  • the year check against year_key >= 2023, <> 2023 and even LIMIT 2023;
  • an assembly-constituency question answered with statewide SQL (the KI-432 incident).
It also ignores year_key_list, so cross-scheme multi-year SQL with no year filter passes. _verifier_year_complaint_is_false crashes with TypeError on a year list, and drops valid BETWEEN complaints. No other step catches any of this, except the sampled LLM verifier.
The solution
  • Place: exact match (upper and trim), and only in filter positions (WHERE, HAVING, ON) through EQ, IN or LIKE, via _sql_filters_on. Accept lgd_district, lgd_block, UPPER(block_name_raw), and district_name_raw until DATA-02 decides. Accept a LIKE only when its pattern matches the resolved place and no other place at that level, checked with string methods against the district tuple and block catalogue.
  • Year: accept EQ or IN on year_key, an FY label on financial_year(_short), or BETWEEN or >=/<= pairs whose integer set equals the resolved set. Keep the date-range form. Read both year_key and year_key_list.
  • Add an AC branch: assembly_constituency_name or ac_name, case-insensitive. Skip it for Focus Legacy and CM Elevate Legacy, which have their own AC guards.
  • Make _verifier_year_complaint_is_false list-safe and range-aware. Render year_key_list in _entities_block.
Don't do this
  • "Accept a LIKE only with the whole multi-word name". It breaks the pinned lgd_block ILIKE '%tura%' case for TURA MUNICIPAL BOARD.
  • "Range covers the list". It must be set equality, because BETWEEN 2022 AND 2025 is wrong for a single-year question.
